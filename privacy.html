Privacy Policy — RecapHub Uploader

Last updated: October 20, 2026

RecapHub Uploader ("the App") is a single-user, personal tool that its owner uses to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account via TikTok's official Content Posting API. This policy explains exactly what data the App handles.

1. Data the App receives from TikTok

When the owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit (OAuth), TikTok provides the App with: (a) basic account information for the authorizing account (scope user.info.basic — open ID, username, and display name), used only to confirm which account authorized the App; and (b) an OAuth access token and refresh token, used only to call the Content Posting API on that account's behalf.

2. Video files

Video files, titles, and captions that the owner selects for upload are transmitted from the App to TikTok's Content Posting API. The App does not publish, share, or sell these files to any other party, and does not use them for advertising, profiling, or model training.

3. What the App does NOT collect

The App does not collect TikTok passwords (authorization happens entirely on TikTok's own OAuth screens), does not read direct messages, does not access contacts, does not track browsing activity, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not collect data from any person other than the authorizing account owner.

4. Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored securely by the operator and are used solely to perform uploads the owner requested. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings (which immediately stops the App from acting on the account), and can contact the operator to have stored tokens deleted. Basic account information is retained only while the authorization is active.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok, as required to operate the API integration described above. The operator does not sell personal data and does not disclose it to third parties except where required by law.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.
