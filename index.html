RecapHub Uploader

RecapHub Uploader is a personal, single-user tool that lets its owner publish the owner's own recap videos to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, after a one-time OAuth authorization with TikTok Login Kit.

It does not scrape, does not automate browsing or engagement, and cannot access any account other than the one that authorized it.
