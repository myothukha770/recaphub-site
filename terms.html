Terms of Service — RecapHub Uploader

Last updated: October 20, 2026

RecapHub Uploader ("the App") is a personal video-publishing tool operated by the account owner. The App allows its owner to upload the owner's own video files to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, after the owner authorizes the App with TikTok Login Kit (OAuth).

1. Who may use the App

The App is a single-user tool. It is not offered to the public, it has no public registration, and it can only publish to TikTok accounts whose owners have explicitly authorized it through TikTok's OAuth consent screen. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account's settings.

2. Acceptable use

The owner agrees to use the App only to publish content that the owner created or has the rights to publish, and to comply with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times. The App does not scrape content, does not automate browsing, liking, following, or commenting, and does not access any account other than the authorizing owner's account.

3. How publishing works

After OAuth authorization, the App submits the owner's selected video files to TikTok using the Content Posting API scopes granted by the owner (user.info.basic, video.upload, and, where approved by TikTok, video.publish). Videos uploaded with the video.upload scope arrive in the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, and the owner decides whether to publish them inside the TikTok app.
